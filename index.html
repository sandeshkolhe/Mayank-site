A New Chapter for Our Society

Shaping the Future

Our Housing Society, comprising 64 members, occupies a prime 3,588.22 sq. m. CIDCO-leased plot along the Mulund–Airoli Highway. Its location offers excellent redevelopment potential.

The proposed project will create a modern commercial-cum-residential development with quality homes, suitable commercial spaces and essential amenities, while protecting every member's interests.

Redevelopment will improve our living environment, enhance property values and support a better future for all. This website provides transparent, timely updates on project documents, milestones, presentations and decisions.
